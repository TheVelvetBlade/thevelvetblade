A barbershop with a soft spot for the classics

The Velvet Blade is an independent Brighton barbershop with a particular passion for classic barbering — pompadours, slickbacks and traditional short-back-and-sides — alongside skin fades and other modern styles.

Come with a clear idea of what you want, or come with absolutely no idea at all. Either is fine.

Finding the entrance

Look for THE VELVET BLADE BARBERSHOP sign above the passage into St Peter’s Yard. Walk through the passage and head up the yellow-edged stairs.

The shop is upstairs, so unfortunately there is no wheelchair-accessible entrance.
